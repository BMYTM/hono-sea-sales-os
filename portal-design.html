Behind the design · How it works on Microsoft 365

The portal is a SharePoint hub site that connects one global content site with a workspace for each market. Content is stored once, tagged with metadata, and surfaced everywhere through search and filters — so country heads reuse HQ assets instead of rebuilding them.

Architecture

Hub + spoke sites

·Hub: SEA Sales Hub (this portal home & global search)
·Global site: master proposals, pricing, decks, playbooks
·Market sites: PH · ID · MY · SG · TH · VN localised content
Storage

Governed libraries

·One document library per pillar with version history
·Metadata columns: Market, Industry, Deal stage, Status, Owner, Expiry
·Content types enforce approved templates
Access & control

Permissions & trust

·M365 groups: Everyone (read) · Country teams (edit) · Sales Ops (approve)
·Approval flow + "latest approved" labels via Power Automate
·Auto-archive on the Expiry date to kill stale collateral
Author in Global site→ Sales Ops approves→ Tagged & published to Hub→ Country teams reuse & localise→ Usage & win/loss feeds back
